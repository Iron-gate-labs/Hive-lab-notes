Documentation, experiments, hardware notes, and local AI research from the Iron Gate laboratory.

IRON GATE LABS

The Hive Laboratory

Independent computing. Local intelligence. Open experimentation.

Iron Gate Labs is an independent computing laboratory focused on local AI, computer hardware, software experimentation, reverse engineering, legacy computing, and digital independence.

At the center of the laboratory is Hive — a locally operated computing environment designed to explore what can be accomplished with accessible hardware, open-source software, and self-hosted intelligence.

What We Do

* Local AI: Running, testing, and optimizing locally hosted language models.
* Hardware Research: GPU experimentation, performance testing, repurposing and upgrading hardware.
* Software Engineering: Building tools, applications, and experimental systems.
* Reverse Engineering: Understanding legacy software, systems, and hardware.
* Digital Independence: Exploring alternatives to cloud-dependent computing.
* Laboratory Documentation: Publishing experiments, discoveries, failures, and technical notes.

The Hive

Hive is our primary experimental computing platform.

It serves as a foundation for local AI inference, development, automation, and experimental software engineering.

We believe useful computing infrastructure doesn’t need to be expensive, proprietary, or dependent on centralized services.

Laboratory Philosophy

1. Experiment before assuming.
2. Document successes and failures.
3. Prefer open standards and open-source software.
4. Make practical use of existing hardware.
5. Share knowledge and encourage independent experimentation.
